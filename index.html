Formula chain

Water flows through: effective token count → model multiplier → water rate (low / mid / high). All three scenarios are always reported — never just one number.

effective_tokens = (output_tokens × 1.000) // autoregressive decode — formula baseline + (fresh_input_tokens × 0.200) // pricing proxy: $3 / $15 MTok + (cache_creation_tokens × 0.250) // pricing proxy: $3.75 / $15 MTok + (cache_read_tokens × 0.001) // floor value — see note below scaled_tokens = effective_tokens × model_multiplier water_mL = scaled_tokens × rate // three scenarios

Empirical anchor

Li et al. 2023: ChatGPT consumes ~500 mL per 20–50 simple conversations in the US.
Reverse: 500 mL ÷ 30 conversations ÷ 1,200 tokens/conversation = 0.014 mL/token (mid rate).

Note on cache_read weight (0.001×)

The pricing proxy gives ~0.02× ($0.30/$15 MTok). At that weight, cache reads dominate long-context Claude Code sessions — 8.7 M cache tokens produce 2.4 L, contradicting the 500 mL / 30-conversation anchor. The 0.001 floor prevents dominance while acknowledging that some compute is involved. A v2 formula will introduce a cache_size × output_tokens cross-term. The deviation from the pricing proxy is documented in formula.json and absorbed by the ±50% accuracy band.
